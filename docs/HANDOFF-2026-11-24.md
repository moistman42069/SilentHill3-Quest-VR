## Design decisions
- Stereo: multiview (GL_OVR_multiview2) in sh3-nx's renderer: one pass, both eyes. Every 3D vertex stage ends with
  gl_Position = uEye[gl_ViewID_OVR] * p, uEye = P_eye * T_eye * inverse(P_game) (the game's projection, widened so
  nothing is culled at the eyes' edges). Pre-transformed (rhw) 2D goes to the HUD layer. Render targets the scene
  passes through become 2-layer arrays and are sampled per view.
- GLES: no glClipControl there; the vertex stage flips y and maps depth (z' = 2z - w) and the front face swaps;
  the eye images are flipped by XR_FB_composition_layer_image_layout (no extra copy).
- First person: our own camera control in the recompiled game (the PC VR mod used OTSMod.dll's memory).
- Input: sh3-nx's DirectInput pad is fed from OpenXR (the game keeps its own input logic).
- PC test build first (Windows, desktop GL) to check everything here; the Android build reuses all of it.
