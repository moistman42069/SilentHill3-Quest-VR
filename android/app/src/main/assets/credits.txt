SH3 Quest VR is built directly on other people's work.

cutarev: sh3-nx (and sh2-nx), the port underneath: the recompiled game and its runtime.
NotGodlikeUwU: the Silent Hill 3 VR mod for PC, whose findings (weapons in the hand, their calibration, physical
melee through the game's own hit volumes, aiming, movement, world scale, the drifting fog's far layer, the picture
settings) are taken and refined here.
heathercherymason-beep: SH3AndroidEvolved, sh3-nx on Android.
sp00nznet: xboxrecomp, the recompiler (MIT).
ThirteenAG: WidescreenFixesPack (MIT).
zealottormunds: sh3cammod, the first-person camera idea.
Steam006: the Silent Hill 3 PC Fix.
The Khronos Group: OpenXR and OpenGL ES.
SDL: SDL2.
Dominic Szablewski: pl_mpeg, the movies' decoder (MIT).
Our Ratchet & Clank and Jak and Daxter Quest VR ports: the headset layer and this launcher.
