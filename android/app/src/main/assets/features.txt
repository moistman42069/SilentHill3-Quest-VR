Dynamic cutscenes (on)
In a cutscene you see through Heather's eyes while the scene shows her, and from the game's camera when it shows
something else or her face up close. For steady third-person cutscenes, always from the game's camera: Options, View,
"Cutscenes", At the camera. The same row also offers first person all the way, or a cinema screen.

Your hands are hers
Her arms, hands and fingers follow yours. Her fingers stop on walls and objects; close your hand on an item, a handle
or a door to take or use it. Turn your left wrist toward you and look at her watch: her health and stamina show there
("Health and stamina on your wrist", View, turns them off).
A ghost of her hand where yours goes into a wall is on the VR menu (off to start). Under the rooms' lamps her shadow
follows her body as you move it, the weapon in her hand too.
Your head in a wall darkens the view; held there 2 seconds, the view goes back to Heather.

Weapons
Grip at your right hip for your gun, over your right shoulder for your melee weapon ("Gun on the hip, melee on the
shoulder" off swaps them); let go there to put it back. "Weapon holster radius" and "Ammo pouch radius" set how near
your hand must come. Supplies
are in the pouch at your belt, the flashlight in your chest pocket. Swing to hit; hold a weapon or your forearm up
between you and a monster to block. Reload by hand: right stick down drops the magazine, take a new one from the front
of your belt and push it in, then rack the slide. When a monster knocks you down or grabs you, shake your controllers to
get free. The first time something new comes up a short hint shows once in front of you; the VR menu's Hints page
lists every hint to read again.

Controls
Left stick walks (click it to run until you click again or let go: Sprint toggle), right stick turns. The main trigger fires; melee weapons you swing. A is the action
button, X the map, Y the inventory, B the supplies. A short press of the left menu button pauses; hold it for a second
for the VR menu, anywhere (in play the game pauses under it). First or third person: the VR menu's View page, "First
person". Both sticks clicked together recentre the view. The Controls tab has the whole list.

Comfort
Smooth turning (or snap), seated play and your height are on the Options tab, Comfort, with a vignette while
turning if you want one.

Picture and performance
120 Hz: the headset redraws your head's turns 120 times a second, and a frame the game is late with waits the least.
Updating to 0.1.6 sets every Performance option to its default once. The game's own time step is taken from how long each frame really shows, so scenes stay
in step with their voices at any refresh rate. Render resolution 1.0x (the headset's own; higher on the
Options tab if your headset has room), foveated rendering at Medium, and Dynamic resolution drawing the 3D picture a
little smaller for a moment if the headset starts falling behind (and back again if that does not help). Both eyes
are drawn together in one pass: each of the game's draws goes to both eyes at once, so the work of a frame is done
once. Smoother frames (AppSW) is there, off to start: it needs the game to keep up with half the refresh rate.

The picture
No screen filters to start: no film grain (neither of the game's two), no drifting fog, no sharpening, no smoothing
and no vignette; the picture as the game draws it. No lens flares, depth of field or shaking double picture (they are
the camera's, not the world's). The game's effects sit at their place in the world, facing you, the muzzle flash at
the muzzle; in first person the lamps' glows are left out, as the PC VR mod leaves them (their bulbs still glow).
Film grain, Drifting fog (far off in the world, not in front of your eyes), Smooth edges, Brightness, Contrast, Color saturation and Darker edges
are on the Options tab, View, or in the VR menu in the game.

Every VR feature is on from the start except Smoother frames and the ghost hands, the screen
filters off; each one can be changed on the Options tab or in
the VR menu in the game (hold the left menu button for a second).

Your copy of the game
Any PC copy of Silent Hill 3 (2003) should work: North American, European or Asian, CD or DVD, the exe as the disc
installed it (copy protection and all), a no-CD or no-DVD exe, or one changed by a fix such as the SH3 PC Fix. The
port runs its own copy of the game's code and only reads the exe's data, the same in every copy we know of. Not the
PS2 game or the PS3/Xbox 360 HD Collection. If the check is not sure, "Play in VR anyway" still tries it.
