IN PLAY (first person; third person where it differs)
Move: left stick (towards where the head looks, or the left hand: Options, Comfort, Walk towards). Walking in the room moves Heather too.
Turn: right stick (smooth, or snap 45 degrees: Options, Comfort).
Run: left stick click (Sprint toggle on: she runs until you click again or let the stick go; off: hold it in; Options, Comfort).
Action, examine, talk: A (what you look at, when the way Heather faces misses it; a tick and a buzz when it is used). Or reach out and grip.
Ready the weapon: main trigger held (third person: main grip or trigger).
Attack, fire: main trigger. Melee weapons: swing them. Empty hands: punch.
Stomp a monster that is down: stand by it with the trigger released and press A (the game's own stomp or kick).
Flashlight: off-hand trigger, or grip at your chest pocket.
Map: X.   Inventory: Y.   Supplies (health): B, or grip at the belt pouch.
Search view: right stick click.
Strafe (third person): off grip held with the left stick left or right.
Pause, skip a scene: left menu button, a short press.
VR menu (every VR option): hold the left menu button for a second, anywhere; in play the game pauses under it.
First / third person: VR menu, View, First person.
Hints: a short line the first time something new comes up, each shown once; every hint to read again: VR menu, Hints page.
Recentre the view: click both sticks together (a click of one stick alone does what it always did).

WEAPONS (first person)
Draw: main hand to the holster, grip. Your gun (handgun, shotgun, submachine gun, stun gun) on your right hip;
your melee weapon (knife, steel pipe, katana, maul) over your right shoulder ("Gun on the hip, melee on the
shoulder" off swaps them). Each gives back the one you last put there; drawing one puts the one in your hand in its
own holster. Left-handed: the left side. How near your hand must come: "Weapon holster radius" and "Ammo pouch
radius" (Options, Hands and weapons).
Put away: hold it at its holster and let go.
Two hands: off grip on the foregrip of a long gun, the pipe, katana or maul (under the handgun to steady it). Where the
second hand takes hold, per weapon: VR menu, Hands and weapons, "Two-hand grip spot" (Adjust, then take hold where
you want it; or reset the weapon in your hand, or every weapon).
Aim: where the gun points; the red guide dot shows it.
Melee: a real swing hits where the weapon went. Punches land half the weakest weapon's hit; a gun swung hard hits like
a pipe.
Block: hold the weapon, or your forearm, up between you and a monster (Options, Hands and weapons).
Health and stamina: in first person, turn your left wrist toward you and look at her watch (third person: the game's HUD; "Health and stamina on your wrist" off, View: the game's HUD in first person too).
Get free when knocked down or held: shake both controllers hard (Options, Hands and weapons), or press buttons fast, or push the left stick side to side.
The purple break-free bar shows how far along you are ("Break-free bar when grabbed", View). Hitting the monster
that holds her breaks its hold.

RELOAD (Manual reload, on)
Drop the magazine: right stick down. A new one: left hand to the ammo pouch at the front of your belt, grip; push it
up into the bottom of the grip until it seats. Shotgun: shells one at a time from the pouch into the port under the gun.
Then, if the gun was empty: pull the handgun's slide (or the submachine gun's knob, on top at the back) all the way
back and let go; pump the shotgun back and forward. Manual reload off: the game reloads by itself.

MENUS, MAP, MEMOS, PUZZLES
Point with either controller (the last trigger pulled) and pull the trigger to choose; or the left stick and A.
Back: B. Map zoom: right grip.
VR menu: hold the left menu button for a second. In it: point and pull the trigger (left half of a
value steps down, right half up), or the left stick and A. Close: B, Y or the left menu button; opened in play, a
short press of the left menu button then goes back to play.

Left-handed (Options, Hands and weapons) swaps the hands: weapons, triggers and grips.
