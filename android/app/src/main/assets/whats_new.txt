  picture: the screen fades, as with A. With "Open doors by hand" off (Hands and weapons), closing your hand on a door
  opens it at once, as on 0.1.9. A still opens doors as before.

0.1.4:

- Every PC copy of Silent Hill 3 (2003) should now work: North American, European or Asian, CD or DVD. As far as we
  know they all have the same sh3.exe build, dated 2003-09-03. The launcher no longer asks for one exact sh3.exe. It
  checks the exe's data, the only part of it the game reads (the port runs its own copy of the game's code). These

- A game copied one folder too deep (game/SILENT HILL 3/...) is moved up by itself, and sh3.exe is found however its
  name is written (SH3.EXE too).
- Not sure about your copy? When there is a sh3.exe but the check is not sure, "Play in VR anyway" and "Play flat
  anyway" try it, and the game's log says what it found.
- Smoother frames (AppSW) works now, on from the start. The game gives the headset how the world moved and how far
  away it is, so at 120 Hz the headset makes every other frame itself instead of showing the last one twice: walking
  and turning look smooth. Your hands and what you hold are left still, and cuts are not smeared. Off or on: Options,
  Performance, or the VR menu (from the next start). Updating turns it on once.
